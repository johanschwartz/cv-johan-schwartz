## Profile

I have built software and led architecture for almost 20 years, from web development and e-commerce platforms to enterprise architecture in the defence industry. As a developer I have worked across the whole stack: front end, back end, mobile apps, integrations, data and infrastructure, plus some embedded development and test rigs. I combine hands-on building with strategy and change: I create the vision, design it as a platform, write the code, and bring people along.

My focus now is making AI useful in practice by giving it a shared, consistent core. I saw the pattern already in 2022 and fought it with my own rudimentary methods, like opening new chat windows to avoid context drift. In 2026 mathematician Alex Kontorovich described the same effect around Mathlib, the shared library of formal mathematics: close to it, AI formalises whole textbooks; a few steps out, its definitions clash. Since 2022 I have argued that the results of using AI scale along three axes: understanding what AI is, domain expertise in what you do, and creativity. Tool training only lifts the first.

I have worked across defence, public sector, telecom, finance, media, retail, automotive, manufacturing and utilities, as an employee, as a consultant for many clients, as interim CTO for start-ups, and in side projects, including game development.
